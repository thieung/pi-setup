Pinned in `pi` as:

## Role

Drafts a short "while you were away" recap when you leave a session and shows it when you return. Built for many parallel agents.

## Why it is in pi

Read-only summarization; no orchestration behavior.

## Guardrails

- The recap is generated by a model call, so it consumes a small amount of tokens per session.
